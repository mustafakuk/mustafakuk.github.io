Privacy Policy

TingleTap · Last updated: 8 August 2026

English · Türkçe

This Privacy Policy explains how TingleTap ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is operated by Mustafa Kuk.

Information handled by the app

TingleTap does not require an account. The following information is stored locally on your device:

  • Your haptic, motion and appearance settings.
  • Stress check-ins, session duration and completed-session history.
  • Toy interaction counters, records, selected materials and daily free-use time.

This local wellness and interaction data is not uploaded to us.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to verify subscription status and restore purchases. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, product and transaction information, subscription status, platform information and limited technical diagnostics. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

How information is used

  • To remember your preferences and local progress.
  • To calculate the daily free-use allowance on your device.
  • To verify, provide and restore paid access.
  • To diagnose purchase errors and keep subscription access working.

Third-party services

  • Apple App Store — payment processing and subscription management. See Apple Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — subscription verification and purchase restoration. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

TingleTap does not contain advertising and does not sell personal information.

Data retention and deletion

You can erase local session history, settings and counters from Settings → Reset data. Removing the app also removes its local data. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records as required for purchase history, fraud prevention, accounting and legal compliance. To request deletion of RevenueCat data associated with the app, contact us at the address below.

Security

Purchase-related communication uses encrypted connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's privacy

TingleTap is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum digital-consent age in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above identifies the latest version.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: mstfkuk@gmail.com.

Uygulamanın işlediği bilgiler

RevenueCat anonim bir uygulama kullanıcı kimliği, ürün ve işlem bilgileri, abonelik durumu, platform bilgisi ve sınırlı teknik tanılama verileri işleyebilir. Ödeme kartınızın tam bilgileri bize ulaşmaz.

Bilgileri nasıl kullanırız

  • Tercihlerinizi ve yerel ilerlemenizi hatırlamak için.
  • Günlük ücretsiz kullanım süresini cihazınızda hesaplamak için.
  • Ücretli erişimi doğrulamak, sunmak ve geri yüklemek için.
  • Satın alma hatalarını incelemek ve abonelik erişimini çalışır tutmak için.

Üçüncü taraf hizmetleri

  • Apple App Store — ödeme işleme ve abonelik yönetimi. Apple Gizlilik Politikası.

TingleTap reklam içermez ve kişisel bilgileri satmaz.
