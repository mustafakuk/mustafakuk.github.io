321: Amerikan Futbolu & Basket, iki oyuncunun karşılıklı yarıştığı çevrimiçi bir spor bilgi oyunudur. Tahtada iki takım belirir, ikisinde de forma giymiş bir oyuncu bulunmaya çalışılır. Bu politika hangi verileri topladığımızı, neden topladığımızı ve bunlar üzerinde ne yapabileceğinizi anlatır.

Kısa özet: hesabınızı, seçtiğiniz kullanıcı adını, isterseniz profil fotoğrafınızı, maç istatistiklerinizi ve arkadaş listenizi saklarız. Şifrenizi ve ödeme bilgilerinizi hiçbir zaman görmeyiz. Uygulamada reklam yok, reklam takip kimliğinizi okumuyoruz ve verinizi kimseye satmıyoruz.

Privacy Policy

321: Football & Basketball is an online sports trivia game where two players compete head to head. Two teams appear on the board and you race to name a player who suited up for both. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version: we store your account, the username you choose, your profile photo if you add one, your match statistics, and your friend list. We never see your password or your payment details. There are no ads, we don't read your advertising identifier, and we don't sell your data.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account ID and email address To create your account and restore your progress when you change devices. It comes from you when you sign up with email, or from the provider when you sign in with Google or Apple.
The username you choose Shown publicly to your opponent in matches, on the leaderboard, and in friend lists.
Profile photo (optional) Only if you pick one from your library. It is downscaled, stored with your account, and shown to opponents and friends.
Your chosen sport Whether you play football or basketball. Matchmaking needs it: you only meet players on the same sport.
Game statistics Matches played and won, correct answers, average answer time, rank points, streaks, daily missions, and achievements. Used on your profile and on the weekly and monthly leaderboards.
Room and match records Room code, chosen mode and conference, teams, score, and the players typed. Needed to keep both screens in sync live.
Daily game records Whether you entered each of the day's four games and the score you got. Needed because each game allows one try, and for the daily standings.
Friend list, requests, and invites To add friends, show whether you are online, and let people invite you to a match.
Notification token Only if you allow notifications. So we can tell you when someone joins your room or a friend invites you.
Purchase records To attach the match credits and subscription status you bought to your account. Your card details never reach us.

What we don't collect: your password (Apple, Google, and Firebase handle authentication), card or payment details, your contacts, your location, health data, or your advertising identifier. There are no ads and no third-party ad tracking; we do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

Who processes your data

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, analytics, and notifications. Your account, statistics, and match rooms live on Firebase servers.
  • Sign in with Apple / Sign in with Google — only if you choose those methods. They pass us your name and email address. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only ever see the relay address.
  • RevenueCat — verifies in-app purchases. It receives your account ID and the purchase receipt from Apple.
  • Apple App Store — all payments go through Apple.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone beyond the services above.

Where the servers are

Data is stored on Google Firebase servers in the United States. If you play from Turkey or Europe, your data is transferred to that region.

What is public

Your username, your profile photo if you have one, your rank, and your weekly and monthly win count are visible to everyone using the app. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Player and team data

The pro football and basketball player and team information in the game is a fixed dataset compiled from public roster records and ships inside the app. It is not about you and is never sent anywhere while you play. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any professional sports league, team, or players' association.

Usernames and moderation

The username you choose passes through an automatic filter; names found unsuitable are rejected, and an existing name later found unsuitable may have to be changed. If you see an offensive username, write to us.

Deleting your data

Open the app and go to Profile → Account → Delete my account. We re-verify your identity for safety, then permanently delete your account, your username record, your profile photo, your statistics, your daily game records, your friend connections, and your purchase counters. This cannot be undone.

Past leaderboard records also contain other players' standings, so they are not deleted outright; your row in them is anonymised and detached from your account.

If you cannot reach the app, write to mstfkuk@gmail.com with your username. We complete requests within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

Children

321: Football & Basketball is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Security

Data is stored on Google Firebase behind server-side security rules that restrict who can read and write what. Sign-in is handled by Apple, Google, or Firebase Authentication; we never process your password.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, and delete your data, and to object to its processing. You can delete it yourself from inside the app; for anything else, write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we update the date at the top of the page and, when it matters, tell you inside the app.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, and everything else: mstfkuk@gmail.com
