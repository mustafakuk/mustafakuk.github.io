Privacy Policy

Picklock · Last updated: 7 October 2026

English · Türkçe

This Privacy Policy explains how Picklock ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is operated by Mustafa Kuk.

Information handled by the app

Picklock does not require an account and does not collect personal information. The following game data is stored only on your device:

  • Your best score and your recent scores.
  • Rewards you have unlocked and the cosmetics you have equipped.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

No tracking, no advertising

Picklock contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs. Gameplay works fully offline. The only network requests are made by the purchase service described below. We do not sell or share personal information.

Purchases and subscriptions

Optional cosmetic packs and the Picklock Club subscription are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases, provide subscription access and restore purchases. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, product and transaction information, subscription status, platform information and limited technical diagnostics. We do not receive your name, Apple ID email or payment-card details.

Third-party services

  • Apple App Store — payment processing and subscription management. See Apple Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — purchase verification and restoration. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Deleting Picklock removes all of its local data. Apple and RevenueCat keep transaction records as required for purchase history, fraud prevention, accounting and legal compliance. To request deletion of RevenueCat data associated with the app, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Picklock does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above identifies the latest version.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: mstfkuk@gmail.com.
