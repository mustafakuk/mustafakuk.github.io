DraftWard, Dota 2 oyuncuları için profesyonel maç verisine dayanan bir draft ve build yardımcısıdır. Üst bardaki hero'ları tanır, rakip kadroya göre build önerir ve pro maç arşivini gösterir. Bu politika hangi verilerin nerede durduğunu anlatır.

Kısa özet: DraftWard'da hesap yok. Kayıtların, son oynadığın hero'lar ve günlük kullanım hakkın yalnızca senin cihazında durur. Tarama için seçtiğin görüntüler cihazda işlenir, hiçbir yere gönderilmez. Uygulama internete yalnızca satın alma için bağlanır. Reklam yok, takip yok, veri satışı yok.

Privacy Policy

DraftWard · Last updated: 3 October 2026

Türkçe · English

DraftWard is a draft and build helper for Dota 2 players, built on professional match data. It recognizes the heroes on the top bar, suggests builds for the enemy lineup, and shows a pro match archive. This policy explains where data lives.

The short version: DraftWard has no accounts. Your saved items, recently played heroes and daily usage stay on your device only. Images you pick for a scan are processed on the device and never uploaded. The app only goes online for purchases. No ads, no tracking, no selling data.

What stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app's folder on your phone. It never reaches us and is removed when you delete the app.

DataWhy
Saved heroes, players and matches To show them in the Saved tab.
Recently played heroes and positions To put them first in the hero picker and to guess "which one is you" in a scan.
Daily analysis counter To count the 2 free match analyses per day. Resets at midnight.
Language choice and last known purchase status To open the app in your language and to honour what you bought even when you're offline.

Camera and photos

The camera only opens when you tap "Camera"; picking from your library uses Apple's own picker, and the app only sees the single image you choose. The image is processed on the device to recognize the hero portraits on the top bar. It is not stored, uploaded, or shared.

Purchases

  • Apple App Store — all payments go through Apple. Your card or payment details never reach us. Your subscription status is read from Apple.
  • RevenueCat — to verify purchases and know whether your subscription is active. It receives a randomly generated app user ID that doesn't identify you and the purchase record from Apple; no name, email, or advertising identifier is sent. Details: revenuecat.com/privacy

What we don't collect: name, email, phone number, location, contacts, advertising identifier, usage analytics, or crash reports. There are no ads and no third-party tracking; we do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

Match data

The pro match, player, hero and item information in the app is compiled from OpenDota's public data and ships inside the app. Pro players' in-game names appear in those public match records. This data is not about you and is never sent anywhere while you use the app.

Dota 2 is a trademark of Valve Corporation. DraftWard is not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve.

Deleting your data

Everything on your device is deleted when you delete the app; there is no account. If you want the purchase verification record deleted, write to mstfkuk@gmail.com with your purchase receipt; we complete requests within 30 days.

Children

DraftWard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we update the date at the top of the page and, when it matters, tell you inside the app.
